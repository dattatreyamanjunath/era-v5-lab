# P = 64: what changes when the codec keeps 64 bytes per token

This repeats the P=32 audit ([collisions.md](collisions.md)) with `pos_dim = 64` on the same six tokenizers. It covers the decode table and raw byte table, collisions with causes and script classes, the proof checks, and three determinism runs.

- **Config:** [`configs/p64.yaml`](configs/p64.yaml).
- **Results:** [`results/p64/`](results/p64/), rendered in [`results/p64/tables.md`](results/p64/tables.md).
- **Side-by-side comparison:** [`results/compare_p32_p64.md`](results/compare_p32_p64.md), generated by `python -m kron_audit compare results/p32 results/p64`.

**Every check passed at P=64:**
- Lemma 1, Lemma 2 and Theorem 1 on all 921,161 rows of both tables;
- `dynamic` equals `cached` bit for bit;
- all 18 determinism hashes match across 3 fresh CPU processes.

## Results in brief

1. **Truncation collisions roughly halve, and every non-Latin one disappears.** At P=64 every Bengali, Devanagari, Tamil, Thai, Malayalam, Gujarati, Georgian and CJK truncation collision is gone. What is left are long runs (spaces, `----`, `////`, `/****`, `####`) plus one GPT-2 mojibake pair.
2. **U+FFFD collisions don't change.** They come from `decode`, not from P. The decode table still loses over 1,000 IDs per byte-level tokenizer, so the raw byte table matters as much at P=64 as at P=32.
3. **Gemma-3 has no truncation left.** None of its tokens is longer than 64 bytes. Its 110 byte-fallback twins remain, since they have identical bytes at any P.
4. **The paper's "truncated tokens still get distinct embeddings" still fails** for five of the six tokenizers. Fewer tokens are affected, and they are all long symbol and space runs.
5. **The cost doubles.** D goes from 8,192 to 16,384, so the projection matrix doubles (6.3 M → 12.6 M parameters at `d_model = 768`), and so does the byte buffer.

**The one U+FFFD change (DeepSeek, 37 → 36 groups).** IDs 70766 and 71586 are long Myanmar tokens (34 and 37 bytes) whose first byte is a stray continuation byte, which `decode` turns into `�`.
- At P=32, both decode rows are cut to the same 30 bytes (`�ျေးရွာအုပ`). The cause rules test U+FFFD before truncation, so the group was labelled `utf8_replacement`, though truncation was also needed.
- At P=64 the rows differ, so the group splits.

Every other U+FFFD group is identical at both P.

## What happens to the P=32 truncation groups

| tokenizer | truncation groups at P=32 | fully split at P=64 | partly split | unchanged |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| gpt2 | 7 | 6 | 1 | 0 |
| llama-3.2 | 28 | 10 | 15 | 3 |
| qwen3 | 25 | 7 | 15 | 3 |
| gemma-3 | 49 | 49 | 0 | 0 |
| deepseek-v3 | 14 | 9 | 4 | 1 |
| gpt-oss | 29 | 18 | 10 | 1 |

- **Fully split:** every word-like group, e.g. Gemma's Bengali "university" family and gpt-oss's Georgian and Thai words. Their members are 30–64 bytes long, so 64 bytes is enough to tell them apart. The one exception is GPT-2's `ÃÂ` mojibake run, which has 64- and 128-byte versions.
- **Partly split or unchanged:** run families with versions of 64 bytes and longer, such as Llama-3.2 and Qwen3's space runs and `/****…` comment banners. At P=64, every version of 64 bytes or more still shares one row.

## Cost of P=64

|  | P=32 | P=64 |
|---|---|---|
| D = 256·P | 8,192 | 16,384 |
| codec row, fp32 | 32 KiB | 64 KiB |
| projection params, d_model = 768 | 6.3 M | 12.6 M |
| projection params, d_model = 4096 | 33.6 M | 67.1 M |
| byte buffer, gemma-3 (largest V) | 8.9 MB | 17.3 MB |
| audit wall time on this laptop: analysis + 3 determinism runs | 10.3 + 4.2 min | 15.9 + 7.6 min |

**What doubling P buys.** At P=32 the raw byte table loses 775 IDs across all six tokenizers; at P=64 it loses 392. P=64 frees 383 more IDs — mainly the run families, Gemma's Indic words, and gpt-oss's Thai, Georgian and Malayalam words. The price is twice the projection parameters at every model width.

Against that, switching from the decode table to the raw table frees thousands of IDs at either P. That costs nothing at training time, so it is the first fix to make.
